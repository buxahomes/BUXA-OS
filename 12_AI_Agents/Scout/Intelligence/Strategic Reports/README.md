# Strategic Reports

> Strategic Reporting Repository of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Strategic Intelligence

---

# Overview

Strategic Reports is the official repository for long-term strategic assessments produced by Scout.

It stores strategic reports that support executive decision-making and Business Operating System evolution.

Unlike operational reports, Strategic Reports focus on organisational direction, capability development and sustainable business growth.

---

# Purpose

Strategic Reports exists to preserve strategic knowledge generated by Scout.

Every report should help answer one or more strategic questions, including:

- Where is the market going?
- Where should BUXA compete?
- What capabilities should BUXA develop?
- What risks require executive attention?
- What opportunities deserve investment?

---

# Report Principles

Every Strategic Report should be:

- Evidence-based
- Long-term focused
- Cross-functional
- Actionable
- Consistent with the Business Operating System

Strategic reports should support decision-making rather than simply describe information.

---

# Typical Report Categories

Examples include:

- Strategic Opportunity Reports
- Strategic Risk Assessments
- Industry Outlook Reports
- Business Capability Reviews
- Annual Strategic Reviews
- Market Expansion Assessments
- Technology Impact Reports
- Executive Briefings

Additional report types may be introduced as the Business Operating System evolves.

---

# Report Lifecycle

Strategic Reports typically follow the following lifecycle:

Research

↓

Analysis

↓

Recommendation

↓

Executive Review

↓

Decision

↓

Knowledge Integration

↓

Business Operating System Update

Strategic knowledge should improve organisational capability after every completed cycle.

---

# Relationship with Strategic Intelligence

Strategic Intelligence produces strategic recommendations.

Strategic Reports preserve those recommendations as organisational knowledge.

Together they support continuous strategic learning across the Business Operating System.

---

# Philosophy

Every report represents a strategic learning opportunity.

Reports should not exist to document history.

They should exist to improve future decisions.

The value of a Strategic Report is measured by the organisational capability it creates.
