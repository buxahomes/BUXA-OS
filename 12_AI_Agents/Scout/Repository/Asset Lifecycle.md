# Asset Lifecycle

> Asset Lifecycle of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Repository

---

# Overview

Asset Lifecycle defines how organisational assets are created, approved, maintained and retired within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that every organisational asset is properly governed throughout its entire lifecycle, from initial creation to final archival.

Organisational assets should evolve through controlled governance rather than unmanaged accumulation.

---

# Purpose

Asset Lifecycle exists to provide a consistent lifecycle for organisational assets.

Its objectives are to:

- Standardise asset development.
- Support repository governance.
- Protect organisational value.
- Enable controlled asset evolution.
- Reduce obsolete assets.
- Strengthen long-term organisational capability.

A consistent lifecycle ensures that repository assets remain valuable, current and reusable.

---

# Lifecycle Principles

Every organisational asset should follow these principles.

## Purpose Before Creation

Every asset should be created to satisfy a recognised organisational need.

Assets without a defined purpose should not enter the Repository.

---

## Governance Before Publication

Assets should complete the appropriate review and approval process before becoming organisational assets.

Governance establishes organisational trust.

---

## Reuse Before Recreation

Existing approved assets should be reused whenever possible.

Creating duplicate assets should be avoided.

---

## Maintenance Before Obsolescence

Approved assets should be reviewed and updated to remain accurate and relevant.

Unmaintained assets gradually lose organisational value.

---

## Controlled Change Before Unmanaged Growth

Asset changes should be documented, reviewed and version controlled.

Controlled evolution strengthens organisational consistency.

---

## Preservation Before Deletion

Assets with historical or governance value should be archived rather than permanently removed.

Organisational history remains an important organisational asset.

# Lifecycle Stages

## Organisational Need

An identified business requirement creates the need for a new or updated organisational asset.

---

## Asset Creation

The asset is developed according to approved organisational standards.

Documentation should be complete before review begins.

---

## Technical Review

The asset is reviewed for technical accuracy, completeness and consistency.

Technical issues should be resolved before business review.

---

## Business Review

The asset is evaluated against organisational objectives, governance requirements and business value.

---

## Approval

Authorised reviewers approve the asset for organisational use.

Only approved assets may enter the Repository.

---

## Repository Publication

The approved asset becomes an official organisational asset within the Repository.

Metadata, ownership and version information should be recorded.

---

## Operational Use

The asset is used by people, AI systems and organisational processes.

Operational feedback supports future improvement.

---

## Periodic Review

Assets should be reviewed periodically to confirm they remain current, accurate and relevant.

Review frequency should reflect business needs.

---

## Revision

Approved improvements are incorporated through controlled version management.

Revision strengthens long-term organisational capability.

---

## Archive

Assets that are no longer actively used but retain historical or governance value should be archived.

Archived assets remain accessible for reference when appropriate.

---

## Retirement

Assets that no longer provide organisational value may be formally retired following approved governance procedures.

Retirement should preserve traceability.

# Success Criteria

Asset Lifecycle succeeds when organisational assets are:

- Purpose-driven.
- Properly governed.
- Current.
- Version controlled.
- Reusable.
- Well maintained.
- Appropriately archived.
- Fully traceable.

Success is measured by the sustained organisational value of repository assets throughout their lifecycle.

# Philosophy

Organisational assets are created to solve business problems.

Their value increases through responsible governance.

Well-managed assets become long-term organisational capability.

Poorly managed assets become organisational burden.

Asset Lifecycle exists to ensure that every organisational asset is intentionally created, responsibly maintained and appropriately retired.

Every approved asset strengthens organisational capability.

Every controlled revision strengthens organisational learning.

Every preserved asset strengthens the future of the Business Operating System.
