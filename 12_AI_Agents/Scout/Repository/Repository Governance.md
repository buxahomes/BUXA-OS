# Overview

Repository Governance defines the ownership, accountability and governance principles that ensure organisational assets are properly managed within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to establish clear responsibilities for creating, maintaining, approving and protecting organisational assets throughout their lifecycle.

Governance ensures that organisational assets remain trusted, consistent and valuable over time.

---

# Purpose

Repository Governance exists to establish accountability for organisational assets.

Its objectives are to:

- Define asset ownership.
- Clarify organisational responsibilities.
- Protect organisational assets.
- Support repository consistency.
- Strengthen governance transparency.
- Enable sustainable organisational capability.

Clear governance transforms repository assets into trusted organisational resources.

---

# Governance Principles

Repository Governance follows these principles.

## Ownership Before Responsibility

Every organisational asset should have a clearly identified owner.

Ownership provides accountability throughout the asset lifecycle.

---

## Accountability Before Authority

Responsibilities should be clearly defined before governance authority is exercised.

Governance depends upon accountability rather than hierarchy.

---

## Governance Before Growth

Repository expansion should occur through approved governance rather than uncontrolled accumulation.

Quality is more valuable than quantity.

---

## Transparency Before Assumption

Ownership, approval status and lifecycle information should be visible to authorised users.

Transparency supports organisational trust.

---

## Stewardship Before Control

Repository governance exists to preserve organisational value rather than restrict organisational use.

Good governance enables responsible collaboration.

---

## Continuous Responsibility

Governance continues throughout the entire asset lifecycle.

Responsibility does not end after publication.

# Governance Roles

## Repository Owner

Responsible for the long-term integrity and governance of repository assets.

Responsibilities include:

- Defining repository policies.
- Maintaining repository structure.
- Protecting repository integrity.
- Supporting long-term capability.

---

## Asset Owner

Responsible for the ongoing accuracy, relevance and maintenance of an approved organisational asset.

Responsibilities include:

- Maintaining asset quality.
- Initiating updates.
- Managing lifecycle activities.
- Responding to organisational changes.

---

## Asset Contributor

Responsible for creating or improving organisational assets according to approved standards.

Contributors should follow established repository governance and documentation requirements.

---

## Technical Reviewer

Responsible for evaluating technical quality, completeness and consistency.

Technical Review supports asset reliability before approval.

---

## Business Reviewer

Responsible for confirming business value, organisational alignment and operational relevance.

Business Review ensures assets support organisational objectives.

---

## Approver

Responsible for granting formal approval for repository publication.

Only authorised approvers may approve organisational assets.

---

## Repository User

Responsible for using repository assets appropriately and reporting improvement opportunities.

Repository users contribute to continuous organisational learning.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

Repository Governance is responsible for:

- Defining repository ownership.
- Protecting organisational assets.
- Supporting governance consistency.
- Maintaining repository integrity.
- Enabling responsible collaboration.
- Supporting Business Operating System governance.

Governance protects organisational capability while enabling organisational growth.

---

# Governance Controls

Repository Governance may include controls such as:

- Defined ownership.
- Approval authority.
- Version management.
- Access permissions.
- Change management.
- Review schedules.
- Archive policies.
- Audit records.

Governance controls ensure repository assets remain trusted and properly managed.

---

# Success Criteria

Repository Governance succeeds when organisational assets are:

- Clearly owned.
- Properly governed.
- Responsibly maintained.
- Consistently approved.
- Fully traceable.
- Reliably protected.

Success is measured by governance effectiveness rather than repository size.
