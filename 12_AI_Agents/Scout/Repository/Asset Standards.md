# Asset Standards

> Asset Standards of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Repository

---

# Overview

Asset Standards defines the organisational requirements that determine whether information, documentation or other organisational outputs qualify as approved organisational assets within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that every repository asset is reliable, reusable, governed and capable of delivering long-term organisational value.

Not every document is an organisational asset.

Every organisational asset must satisfy defined standards.

---

# Purpose

Asset Standards exists to establish consistent requirements for organisational assets.

Its objectives are to:

- Define asset quality requirements.
- Ensure organisational consistency.
- Improve asset reuse.
- Protect organisational intellectual property.
- Support repository governance.
- Strengthen long-term organisational capability.

Asset Standards ensures that only approved organisational assets become part of the Repository.

---

# Asset Principles

Every organisational asset should follow these principles.

## Value Before Storage

Assets should provide identifiable organisational value.

Storage alone does not justify repository inclusion.

---

## Reusability Before Duplication

Assets should be designed for repeated organisational use.

Duplicate assets reduce consistency and increase maintenance effort.

---

## Governance Before Publication

Assets should be reviewed and approved before becoming organisational assets.

Governance establishes organisational trust.

---

## Traceability Before Adoption

Every asset should be traceable to its owner, version and approval history.

Traceability supports accountability.

---

## Maintainability Before Growth

Assets should be maintained throughout their lifecycle.

Outdated assets reduce organisational capability.

---

## Consistency Before Convenience

Every asset should align with approved organisational standards, terminology and documentation practices.

Consistency enables reliable organisational use.

---

# Asset Requirements

Every approved organisational asset should satisfy the following requirements.

## Approved

The asset has completed the appropriate organisational review and approval process.

---

## Documented

The asset includes sufficient documentation to support correct organisational use.

---

## Version Controlled

The asset maintains identifiable version information.

Version control supports continuous improvement.

---

## Traceable

The asset records ownership, approval status and change history.

---

## Reusable

The asset is suitable for repeated organisational use.

---

## Discoverable

The asset can be located through the repository structure and organisational metadata.

---

## Governed

The asset is managed according to approved repository governance.

---

## Maintainable

The asset can be updated without compromising organisational consistency.

---

# Standard Application

Asset Standards apply to approved organisational assets including:

- Business standards.
- Product Passports.
- Product documentation.
- PDMS documentation.
- Standard operating procedures.
- Templates.
- Reports.
- Research deliverables.
- Training materials.
- Brand assets.
- AI prompts.
- AI workflows.
- Technical documentation.
- Reference resources.

Every repository asset should satisfy the same organisational standards regardless of asset type.

---

# Asset Validation

Before becoming an approved organisational asset, every asset should be validated to confirm that it:

- Provides organisational value.
- Satisfies asset requirements.
- Supports business objectives.
- Can be governed effectively.
- Is suitable for long-term organisational use.

Validation should be objective, repeatable and evidence-based.

---

# Asset Responsibilities

Asset Standards is responsible for:

- Defining repository asset requirements.
- Supporting asset governance.
- Improving organisational consistency.
- Enabling asset reuse.
- Protecting organisational intellectual property.
- Supporting Business Operating System governance.

Asset Standards establishes a common foundation for every approved organisational asset.

---

# Success Criteria

Asset Standards succeeds when organisational assets are:

- Approved.
- Documented.
- Version controlled.
- Traceable.
- Reusable.
- Discoverable.
- Governed.
- Maintainable.

Success is measured by the long-term organisational value of repository assets rather than the number of stored files.
