# Research Operations

> Research Operations of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Operations

---

# Overview

Research Operations defines how Scout conducts structured research across the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that every research activity is systematic, evidence-based and aligned with organisational objectives.

Research is the foundation of every intelligence capability.

Well-structured research produces reliable knowledge, stronger recommendations and better long-term organisational decisions.

---

# Purpose

Research Operations exists to standardise how Scout investigates questions, gathers evidence and develops organisational understanding.

Its objective is not to collect more information, but to discover information that improves business capability.

Every research activity should contribute to the continuous evolution of the Business Operating System.

---

# Objectives

Research Operations helps Scout:

- Understand the research objective.
- Identify reliable evidence.
- Evaluate information quality.
- Collect relevant knowledge.
- Support intelligence analysis.
- Reduce unsupported assumptions.
- Strengthen organisational learning.

---

# Research Principles

Every research activity should follow these principles.

## Purpose Before Research

Research begins with a clearly defined business question.

Information without purpose creates organisational noise.

---

## Evidence Before Opinion

Research should prioritise verifiable evidence over assumptions or personal opinions.

---

## Quality Before Quantity

Collect only information that contributes to organisational understanding.

More information does not necessarily produce better decisions.

---

## Multiple Sources Before Conclusions

Important conclusions should be supported by multiple reliable sources whenever possible.

Cross-validation improves confidence.

---

## Organisational Value Before Curiosity

Research should contribute to business capability rather than satisfy isolated curiosity.

---

## Knowledge Before Repetition

Approved organisational knowledge should always be reviewed before conducting new external research.

---

# Research Lifecycle

Research follows a structured operational cycle.

Research Request

↓

↓

Collect Evidence

↓

↓

Identify Insights

↓

Research is complete only when new understanding has been integrated into organisational capability.

# Evidence Evaluation

Every source should be evaluated for:

- Relevance
- Reliability
- Accuracy
- Timeliness
- Authority
- Consistency

Evidence quality should always be considered before evidence quantity.

# Key Responsibilities

Research Operations is responsible for:

- Defining research objectives.
- Conducting structured research.
- Identifying reliable sources.
- Evaluating evidence quality.
- Supporting intelligence activities.
- Preserving research integrity.
- Contributing to organisational learning.

---

# Success Criteria

Research Operations succeeds when it enables Scout to:

- Produce reliable evidence.
- Reduce unsupported assumptions.
- Improve intelligence quality.
- Strengthen recommendations.
- Expand organisational knowledge.
- Support long-term decision-making.

Success is measured by the quality of organisational understanding rather than the volume of information collected.

---

# Relationship with Other Operations

Research Operations initiates the operational cycle.

Analysis Operations transforms research into understanding.

Knowledge Operations integrates approved knowledge.

Recommendation Operations develops strategic guidance.

Reporting Operations communicates organisational insights.

Continuous Improvement captures learning and strengthens future research capability.

Together these operations establish a complete research-to-learning system.

---

# Philosophy

Research is not the collection of information.

Research is the disciplined pursuit of understanding.

Every question deserves a clear objective.

Every conclusion requires reliable evidence.

Every insight should strengthen organisational capability.

Research Operations exists to ensure that learning is intentional, structured and continuously valuable to the Business Operating System.
