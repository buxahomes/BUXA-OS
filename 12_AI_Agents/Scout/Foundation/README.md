# Foundation

> Identity, Capability and Governing Principles of the Scout Agent

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: BUXA Business Operating System (BOS)

---

# Overview

Foundation defines the permanent identity of the Scout Agent.

It establishes who Scout is, what Scout is responsible for, and the governing principles that every capability, workflow and future enhancement must follow.

Unlike operational documents that evolve over time, the Foundation layer is designed to remain stable and provide long-term architectural consistency across the Business Operating System.

Every future capability of Scout must align with the Foundation before it can become part of the Business Operating System.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Foundation layer is to provide a stable framework for the continuous evolution of Scout.

It defines:

- Identity
- Responsibilities
- Governing Principles

Together these documents ensure that Scout evolves consistently without losing alignment with the long-term objectives of BUXA.

---

# Architecture

The Foundation layer consists of three permanent documents.

## Scout.md

Defines the identity of Scout.

It answers:

- Who is Scout?
- Why does Scout exist?
- What is Scout's mission?
- What is Scout's position within the Business Operating System?

---

## Responsibilities.md

Defines the capability architecture of Scout.

It answers:

- What does Scout do?
- What business capabilities does Scout support?
- What deliverables does Scout produce?
- What responsibilities belong to Scout?

---

## Constitution.md

Defines the governing principles of Scout.

It answers:

- What principles must never be violated?
- What ethical boundaries exist?
- What authority governs Scout?
- How should Scout evolve?

# Design Principles

The Foundation follows several core design principles.

## Stable

The Foundation should change only when the long-term direction of BUXA changes.

---

## Organisational

The Foundation defines organisational capability rather than implementation details.

---

## Platform Independent

The Foundation applies regardless of platform, workflow or technology.

---

## Standards Driven

Every capability must originate from approved Business Standards rather than temporary prompts or individual preferences.

---

## Human Governed

Critical business decisions, permanent knowledge and Business Standards always remain under human authority.

---

# Scope

The Foundation defines permanent architecture only.

It does not define:

- Operational workflows
- Content production processes
- Temporary projects
- Platform-specific guidance
- Marketing campaigns
- Daily execution

These belong to other layers of the Business Operating System.

---

# Long-Term Evolution

The Foundation is expected to remain stable while Scout continues to evolve.

New capabilities may be added.

New workflows may be introduced.

New AI technologies may emerge.

However, every future enhancement must remain consistent with the identity, responsibilities and governing principles established by the Foundation.

---

# Philosophy

Identity provides direction.

Responsibilities define capability.

Principles establish boundaries.

Together they form the permanent foundation of Scout.

A stronger Foundation enables a stronger Business Operating System.

A stronger Business Operating System enables a stronger BUXA.
