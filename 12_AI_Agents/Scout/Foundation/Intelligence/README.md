# Intelligence

> Strategic Intelligence Layer of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Foundation

---

# Overview

The Intelligence Layer is responsible for transforming external information into strategic organisational capability.

It enables Scout to continuously observe the business environment, analyse emerging developments, identify opportunities and risks, and provide actionable recommendations that strengthen the BUXA Business Operating System.

Rather than collecting information for its own sake, the Intelligence Layer converts validated intelligence into reusable knowledge that supports long-term business growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Intelligence Layer is to help BUXA understand the world before making decisions.

It provides structured intelligence that supports:

- Business strategy
- Brand development
- Product evolution
- Marketing direction
- Organisational learning
- AI-Native transformation

The Intelligence Layer ensures that every important recommendation is grounded in research, analysis and approved organisational standards.

# Intelligence Principles

All intelligence produced by Scout follows several core principles.

## Evidence Before Assumption

Recommendations should be supported by reliable evidence rather than speculation.

---

## Context Before Conclusion

Information should always be interpreted within the broader business context.

---

## Long-Term Thinking Before Short-Term Optimisation

Recommendations should strengthen sustainable organisational capability rather than temporary performance.

---

## Knowledge Before Opinion

Approved organisational knowledge takes priority over personal interpretation.

---

## Opportunity and Risk Together

Every opportunity should be evaluated alongside its associated risks.

---

## Intelligence Must Lead to Action

Research alone does not create value.

Intelligence creates value only when it supports better decisions and stronger organisational capability.
