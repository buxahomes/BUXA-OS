# Quality Review

> Quality Review of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Quality

---

# Overview

Quality Review defines how organisational quality is evaluated within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to determine whether organisational knowledge, intelligence, operations and business outputs satisfy approved quality standards before they are relied upon for organisational use.

Quality Review provides objective evaluation.

It does not replace governance, approval or business decision-making.

---

# Purpose

Quality Review exists to provide a structured and consistent method for evaluating organisational quality.

Its objectives are to:

- Assess organisational outputs.
- Verify alignment with approved standards.
- Identify quality risks.
- Recommend improvements.
- Support informed approval decisions.
- Strengthen organisational trust.

Quality Review determines whether quality expectations have been achieved.

---

# Review Principles

Every quality review should follow these principles.

## Objectivity Before Opinion

Reviews should be based on observable evidence and approved organisational standards.

Personal preferences should not influence review outcomes.

---

## Standards Before Expectations

Every review should evaluate outputs against approved organisational standards rather than informal expectations.

---

## Evidence Before Conclusions

Review findings should be supported by verifiable evidence.

Review decisions should be transparent and traceable.

---

## Consistency Before Convenience

The same quality criteria should be applied regardless of project, department or contributor.

---

## Improvement Before Criticism

The purpose of review is to strengthen organisational capability rather than assign blame.

Review findings should encourage continuous improvement.

---

## Independence Before Approval

Quality Review provides an independent evaluation.

Final approval remains the responsibility of authorised governance.

---

# Review Model

Quality Review follows a structured evaluation process.

Review Request

↓

Identify Applicable Standards

↓

Collect Supporting Evidence

↓

Evaluate Against Standards

↓

Document Findings

↓

Recommend Improvements

↓

Submit Review Results

↓

Support Approval Decision

Quality Review concludes with an evidence-based assessment rather than an approval decision.

---

# Review Scope

Quality Review may evaluate:

## Knowledge

Accuracy, consistency, completeness and governance.

---

## Intelligence

Research quality, analytical rigour and evidence.

---

## Operations

Compliance with approved workflows and operational standards.

---

## Business Outputs

Reports, recommendations, documentation and deliverables.

---

## AI Outputs

Alignment with approved knowledge, organisational standards and business objectives.

---

## System Improvements

Proposed changes to the Business Operating System.

---

# Review Outcomes

Quality Review may conclude that an organisational output is:

## Accepted

Meets all applicable quality standards.

---

## Accepted with Recommendations

Suitable for use while improvement opportunities are identified.

---

## Requires Revision

Does not fully satisfy applicable quality standards and should be revised before approval.

---

## Not Ready

Significant quality concerns prevent organisational adoption.

---

# Review Responsibilities

Quality Review is responsible for:

- Evaluating organisational quality.
- Applying approved quality standards.
- Identifying quality risks.
- Documenting review findings.
- Supporting governance decisions.
- Encouraging continuous improvement.

Quality Review supports organisational assurance rather than organisational control.

---

# Key Responsibilities

Quality Review is responsible for:

- Conducting objective quality evaluations.
- Supporting evidence-based review.
- Maintaining review consistency.
- Improving organisational reliability.
- Supporting approval decisions.
- Strengthening Business Operating System quality.

---

# Success Criteria

Quality Review succeeds when organisational evaluations are:

- Objective.
- Consistent.
- Evidence-based.
- Transparent.
- Repeatable.
- Constructive.

Success is measured by the quality of organisational evaluation and its contribution to improved capability rather than the number of reviews completed.

---

# Relationship with Other Quality Domains

Quality Framework defines the organisational quality model.

Quality Standards define quality expectations.

Quality Assurance embeds quality into organisational processes.

Quality Review evaluates organisational outputs.

Continuous Verification maintains quality over time.

Quality Metrics measures organisational quality performance.

Together these domains establish a complete organisational quality system.

---

# Philosophy

Quality cannot be assumed.

It must be evaluated.

Evaluation requires evidence.

Evidence supports trust.

Trust strengthens organisational confidence.

Quality Review exists to ensure that organisational outputs are assessed fairly, consistently and transparently before becoming trusted business assets.

Every objective review strengthens organisational learning.

Every improvement strengthens organisational capability.

Every trusted outcome strengthens the Business Operating System.
