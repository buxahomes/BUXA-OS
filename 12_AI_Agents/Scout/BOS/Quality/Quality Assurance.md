# Quality Assurance

> Quality Assurance of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Quality

---

# Overview

Quality Assurance defines how organisational quality is built into the Business Operating System through structured processes, approved standards and preventive controls.

Its purpose is to ensure that organisational outputs consistently achieve the required quality before they are delivered or adopted.

Quality should be designed into organisational work rather than inspected after completion.

---

# Purpose

Quality Assurance exists to establish preventive quality practices across the Business Operating System.

Its objectives are to:

- Prevent quality issues before they occur.
- Ensure consistent application of approved standards.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve organisational reliability.
- Support evidence-based business decisions.
- Strengthen long-term organisational capability.

Quality Assurance focuses on building quality into every organisational activity.

---

# Assurance Principles

Every quality assurance activity should follow these principles.

## Prevention Before Detection

Quality should be achieved by preventing defects rather than relying on later correction.

---

## Standards Before Execution

Approved organisational standards should guide every activity before work begins.

---

## Evidence Before Approval

Significant organisational outputs should be supported by appropriate evidence before approval.

---

## Process Before Outcome

Consistently following well-designed processes produces reliable organisational outcomes.

---

## Consistency Before Individual Preference

Quality should depend upon organisational standards rather than individual working styles.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Quality Assurance should evolve through operational learning and organisational improvement.

# Success Criteria

Quality Assurance succeeds when organisational work is:

- Consistently performed.
- Standards-based.
- Evidence-supported.
- Repeatable.
- Reliable.
- Ready for approval with minimal rework.

Success is measured by the reduction of quality issues through prevention rather than correction.

Consistent execution reduces variation.

Reduced variation improves organisational reliability.

Quality Assurance exists to ensure that quality is embedded into every organisational activity rather than inspected after completion.

Every preventive control strengthens confidence.

Every consistent process strengthens capability.

Every improvement strengthens the Business Operating System.

Quality is not something that is added.

It is something that is intentionally built.
