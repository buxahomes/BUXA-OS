# Operations

> Operations Layer of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Foundation

---

# Overview

The Operations Layer defines how Scout performs its work.

While the Foundation establishes identity and Intelligence defines capability, Operations provides the operational model that governs how intelligence is transformed into organisational value.

Operations ensures that Scout performs every task using consistent, repeatable and evidence-based processes.

---

# Purpose

The Operations Layer exists to standardise how Scout researches, analyses, recommends and continuously improves.

Its objective is to ensure that every operation follows approved organisational principles rather than individual working preferences.

---

# Responsibilities

Operations defines how Scout:

- Conducts research
- Performs analysis
- Uses organisational knowledge
- Produces recommendations
- Generates reports
- Continuously improves

These operational capabilities provide consistency across every intelligence domain.

---

# Operating Principles

Every operation should follow these principles.

## Evidence Before Opinion

Recommendations must be supported by evidence.

---

## Knowledge Before Assumption

Approved organisational knowledge takes priority over assumptions.

---

## Consistency Before Speed

Reliable execution is more valuable than rapid execution.

---

## System Before Individual Task

Every activity should strengthen the Business Operating System.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Every completed task should improve future organisational capability.
