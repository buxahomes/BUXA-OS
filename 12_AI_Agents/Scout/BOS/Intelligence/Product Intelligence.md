Version: 1.0

# Overview

Product Intelligence enables Scout to strengthen BUXA's product strategy through continuous research, structured analysis and strategic recommendations.

Its purpose is not to design products directly, but to develop a deep understanding of products, customer needs, industry innovation and market expectations.

By transforming product knowledge into strategic insight, Product Intelligence supports long-term product evolution and strengthens the Business Operating System.

---

# Purpose

Product Intelligence exists to help BUXA build better products through better understanding.

It continuously studies:

- Customer needs
- Product technologies
- Material innovation
- Manufacturing capabilities
- Installation practices
- Product performance
- Market expectations

The objective is to improve long-term product competitiveness while maintaining alignment with the BUXA brand.

# Product Evaluation Principles

Product Intelligence evaluates products using six principles.

## Customer Before Specification

Products should solve customer problems before adding technical complexity.

---

## Quality Before Quantity

A focused, high-quality product portfolio creates greater value than unnecessary expansion.

---

## System Before Individual Product

Products should work together as part of an integrated flooring system rather than as isolated items.

---

## Long-Term Performance Before Short-Term Cost

Product decisions should prioritise durability, reliability and customer satisfaction.

---

## Simplicity Before Complexity

Products should remain easy to understand, specify and install.

---

## Continuous Improvement Before Continuous Expansion

Existing products should be continuously improved before introducing unnecessary new products.
