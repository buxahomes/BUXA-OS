# Knowledge

> Knowledge Layer of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Foundation

---

# Overview

The Knowledge Layer defines how organisational knowledge is structured, governed and maintained within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that knowledge is treated as a strategic organisational asset rather than a collection of disconnected documents.

Knowledge provides the foundation for consistent intelligence, reliable operations and sustainable organisational growth.

---

# Purpose

The Knowledge Layer exists to establish a unified organisational knowledge system for BUXA.

Its objective is to ensure that knowledge is:

- Structured.
- Authoritative.
- Consistent.
- Accessible.
- Continuously improved.

Every intelligence capability and operational activity should be supported by approved organisational knowledge.

---

# Responsibilities

The Knowledge Layer defines how BUXA:

- Organises organisational knowledge.
- Classifies knowledge assets.
- Maintains knowledge consistency.
- Governs knowledge quality.
- Preserves organisational memory.
- Supports continuous organisational learning.

Knowledge belongs to the organisation.

Scout is one of its users.

---

# Knowledge Principles

Every knowledge activity should follow these principles.

## Knowledge Is an Organisational Asset

Knowledge should be managed with the same discipline as any other strategic business asset.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Every organisational concept should have one approved authoritative source.

Conflicting knowledge should be resolved through the Business Operating System.

---

## Approved Knowledge Before External Information

Approved organisational knowledge should always take priority when supporting business decisions.

External information may inform organisational learning but should not replace approved standards.

---

## Consistency Before Convenience

Knowledge should remain consistent across every department, document and AI capability.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Knowledge should evolve through structured governance and organisational learning.

Every improvement should strengthen the Business Operating System.

---

## Human Governance

Permanent organisational knowledge should only be established, revised or retired through appropriate human review and approval.

# Philosophy

Knowledge is more than information.

Knowledge is organisational understanding that has been validated, approved and consistently applied.

The value of knowledge is not determined by how much is stored.

It is determined by how effectively it improves organisational capability.

Every approved standard strengthens consistency.

Every lesson strengthens understanding.

Every improvement strengthens the Business Operating System.

Strong organisations do not rely on individual memory.

They build organisational knowledge that outlives individuals.
