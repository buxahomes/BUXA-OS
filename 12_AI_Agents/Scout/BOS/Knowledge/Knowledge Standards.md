# Knowledge Standards

> Knowledge Standards of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Knowledge

---

# Overview

Knowledge Standards defines the principles, requirements and rules governing organisational knowledge within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that every approved knowledge asset is accurate, consistent and suitable for long-term organisational use.

Knowledge should be treated as an organisational standard rather than individual opinion.

---

# Purpose

Knowledge Standards exists to establish a common standard for creating, approving and maintaining organisational knowledge.

Its objectives are to:

- Ensure knowledge consistency.
- Improve organisational reliability.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Strengthen organisational memory.
- Enable knowledge reuse.
- Support long-term Business Operating System evolution.

Every approved knowledge asset should satisfy the same organisational standards.

---

# Knowledge Principles

Every knowledge asset should follow these principles.

## Accuracy Before Availability

Knowledge should be correct before it is made available for organisational use.

Unverified information should not become approved knowledge.

---

## Consistency Before Convenience

Knowledge should remain consistent across every document, department and AI capability.

Conflicting standards reduce organisational trust.

---

## Authority Before Opinion

Approved organisational knowledge should be based on authorised standards, verified evidence or approved business decisions.

Personal opinions should not become permanent organisational knowledge.

---

## Reusability Before Duplication

Knowledge should be created for repeated organisational use.

Existing approved knowledge should always be referenced before creating new knowledge.

---

## Clarity Before Complexity

Knowledge should be written clearly and consistently.

Complex ideas should be understandable without reducing accuracy.

---

## Business Value Before Volume

Knowledge should improve organisational capability.

Information that provides no ongoing organisational value should not become permanent knowledge.

---

# Knowledge Requirements

Every approved knowledge asset should be:

## Accurate

Factually correct and supported by appropriate evidence.

---

## Complete

Sufficiently comprehensive for its intended purpose.

---

## Consistent

Aligned with approved organisational standards.

---

## Current

Maintained to reflect approved organisational practices.

---

## Relevant

Applicable to current organisational capability.

---

## Traceable

Supported by identifiable sources, standards or approved decisions.

---

# Approved Knowledge Sources

Approved organisational knowledge may originate from:

- Approved Business Standards
- Product Standards
- Product Passports
- PDMS Standards
- Brand Standards
- Organisational Policies
- Approved Research
- Executive Decisions
- Regulatory Requirements
- Verified Technical Documentation

External information should be validated before becoming approved organisational knowledge.

---

# Knowledge Asset Standards

Every knowledge asset should include, where appropriate:

- Clear title
- Defined purpose
- Scope
- Ownership
- Version
- Approval status
- Revision history
- Related knowledge assets

Knowledge should remain understandable and maintainable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Standardisation

Knowledge should use:

- Consistent terminology.
- Approved naming conventions.
- Standard document structures.
- Shared organisational definitions.
- Common formatting where appropriate.

Standardisation improves organisational understanding and AI reliability.

---

# Key Responsibilities

Knowledge Standards is responsible for:

- Defining organisational knowledge requirements.
- Maintaining knowledge consistency.
- Supporting knowledge reuse.
- Improving organisational reliability.
- Strengthening organisational standards.
- Supporting Business Operating System governance.

---

# Success Criteria

Knowledge Standards succeeds when organisational knowledge is:

- Accurate.
- Consistent.
- Reusable.
- Trusted.
- Well governed.
- Continuously valuable.

Success is measured by the quality and reliability of organisational knowledge rather than the quantity of documentation.

# Philosophy

Knowledge becomes valuable only when it can be trusted.

Trust is created through consistency.

Consistency is maintained through standards.

Standards transform individual experience into organisational capability.

The Business Operating System does not grow through collecting information.

It grows through establishing knowledge that is accurate, reusable and consistently applied.

Every approved standard strengthens organisational confidence.

Every trusted knowledge asset strengthens the future of BUXA.
