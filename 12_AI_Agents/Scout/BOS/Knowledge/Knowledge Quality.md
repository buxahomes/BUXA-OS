# Knowledge Quality

> Knowledge Quality of the Scout Strategic Intelligence System

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: Scout Knowledge

---

# Overview

Knowledge Quality defines the criteria used to evaluate and maintain the quality of organisational knowledge within the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to ensure that every approved knowledge asset remains accurate, reliable and valuable for long-term organisational use.

Knowledge quality is essential to organisational trust, operational consistency and effective decision-making.

---

# Purpose

Knowledge Quality exists to establish a consistent framework for evaluating organisational knowledge.

Its objectives are to:

- Maintain trusted organisational knowledge.
- Improve knowledge reliability.
- Support consistent decision-making.
- Reduce organisational risk.
- Enable AI-assisted operations.
- Strengthen the Business Operating System.

Every approved knowledge asset should satisfy the same quality expectations.

---

# Quality Principles

Every knowledge asset should follow these principles.

## Accuracy Before Assumption

Knowledge should be factually correct and supported by appropriate evidence.

Assumptions should never become approved organisational knowledge.

---

## Consistency Before Variation

Knowledge should remain consistent across all organisational standards, documents and AI capabilities.

Consistency builds organisational trust.

---

## Completeness Before Publication

Knowledge should contain sufficient information to fulfil its intended purpose.

Incomplete knowledge increases operational uncertainty.

---

## Clarity Before Complexity

Knowledge should communicate ideas clearly without sacrificing precision.

Clear knowledge improves adoption and reduces misunderstanding.

---

## Currency Before Convenience

Knowledge should reflect current approved organisational practices.

Outdated knowledge should be reviewed, revised or retired.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Knowledge quality should improve through operational experience, structured review and organisational learning.

Quality is maintained through disciplined evolution rather than occasional correction.

---

# Knowledge Quality Criteria

Every approved knowledge asset should be evaluated against the following criteria.

## Accuracy

Information is factually correct and supported by appropriate evidence.

---

## Completeness

The knowledge asset adequately covers its defined scope.

---

## Consistency

Content aligns with approved organisational standards and related knowledge assets.

---

## Clarity

Information is understandable, well structured and free from unnecessary ambiguity.

---

## Relevance

Knowledge supports current organisational needs and business capability.

---

## Currency

Knowledge reflects the latest approved organisational practices and decisions.

---

## Traceability

The origin, approval and revision history of the knowledge asset can be identified.

---

## Reusability

Knowledge can be applied consistently across different organisational contexts.

---

# Quality Review

Knowledge quality should be evaluated:

- Before approval.
- During scheduled reviews.
- Following significant organisational change.
- After major product or process updates.
- Following lessons learned from operations.
- When quality concerns are identified.

Quality review supports continuous organisational confidence.

---

# Quality Improvement

Where quality issues are identified, appropriate actions may include:

- Correcting inaccuracies.
- Expanding incomplete content.
- Updating obsolete information.
- Improving clarity.
- Aligning conflicting standards.
- Revising governance records.

Every quality improvement should strengthen organisational capability.

---

# Quality Indicators

High-quality organisational knowledge is:

- Accurate.
- Complete.
- Consistent.
- Current.
- Relevant.
- Understandable.
- Traceable.
- Reusable.

These characteristics establish organisational confidence and enable reliable AI-supported operations.

- Defining quality criteria.
- Supporting quality review.
- Improving organisational knowledge.
- Maintaining knowledge reliability.
- Strengthening organisational trust.
- Supporting continuous improvement.
- Enhancing Business Operating System capability.

# Success Criteria

Knowledge Quality succeeds when organisational knowledge is:

- Trusted.
- Reliable.
- Consistent.
- Current.
- Well maintained.
- Continuously improving.

Success is measured by the confidence with which organisational knowledge can be applied in business operations and decision-making.

# Philosophy

Knowledge creates value only when it can be trusted.

Trust is built through quality.

Quality is sustained through disciplined review, responsible governance and continuous improvement.

The Business Operating System depends on knowledge that is accurate, consistent and continuously maintained.

Every improvement in knowledge quality strengthens organisational capability.

Every trusted knowledge asset strengthens better decisions.

Better decisions build a stronger organisation.

Knowledge Quality exists to ensure that organisational knowledge remains worthy of that trust.
