# Business Architecture

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Authority: BUXA Business Operating System (BOS)

---

# Purpose

Business Architecture defines how BUXA is designed to operate.

It describes the organisational structure, business capabilities, operating principles and the role of artificial intelligence within the Business Operating System.

Unlike a traditional organisational chart, this architecture focuses on business capability rather than reporting hierarchy.

Its objective is to ensure that people, standards, knowledge and AI work together as one integrated operating system.

---

# Vision

BUXA is building the world's first AI-Native premium flooring and project delivery brand.

Artificial Intelligence is not an external productivity tool.

It is a foundational business capability that supports every core business function.

The long-term objective is to create an organisation that continuously learns, continuously improves and continuously evolves through structured knowledge and intelligent systems.

---

# AI-Native Philosophy

BUXA believes that competitive advantage comes from organisational capability rather than individual experience.

Every improvement should become reusable knowledge.

Every lesson should become a standard.

Every standard should strengthen the Business Operating System.

Artificial Intelligence does not replace professional expertise.

It amplifies it through structured knowledge, approved standards and continuous organisational learning.

BUXA operates according to the following principles.
